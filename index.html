Privacy Policy

App: CJP: Cockroach Janata Party (Karyakarta Game)
Package: com.digitalkumbh.politicsgame
Developer: Subhash Chaudhary
Last updated: 3 October 2026

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device data. It works fully offline.

What the app stores

Your game progress (for example, your character's name, day, money and election results) is saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include this save in your own Google account backup; the developer cannot access it.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files access.
  • No network use: everything the game needs is inside the app, and the app blocks every outside web address, so it makes no network requests.
  • No in-app purchases.

Permissions

Vibrate, for short haptic feedback during play, and Internet, which Android's built-in web view (the component that draws the game) needs to run. The app itself sends and receives nothing over the network.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Satire notice

This game is fictional satire. All names, parties and people in it are made up and are not affiliated with any real political party, politician or government.
